Proportional Reasoning & Rate Verification Challenges

Question 1
A bag of South Africa Candy Apples costs $1.95 for a mass of 500g. What is the cost rate of these apples per kilogram (1kg)?
Question 2
A pack of China Fuji Apples contains 5 apples and costs $3.45. What is the average cost rate per individual apple?
Question 3
A premium box of Korea Shingo Pears weighs 1.5kg and costs $7.95. If a customer wants to buy exactly 3kg of these pears at the same rate, how much will it cost?
Question 4
Pears Bonanza Australia Packham Pears are priced at $4.95 for an 800g bag. What is the cost rate per 100g for these pears?
